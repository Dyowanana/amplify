## 2. Summary
A cream-card carousel of 2 x 3 photo-object grids (what causes a body annoyance, what to swap in, what fixes it) that reframes the problem as "water or habit, not fat", explains the mechanism on a text slide with a first-person app plug, and closes on a warm lifestyle photo with the outcome and the bio link.

## 3. Best for
- Everyday body annoyances with simple, food-and-habit causes: puffiness, bloating, water retention, digestion, cravings, sleep, skin.
- Brands that sell a habit or routine helper (an app, a service, a programme) and want the plug to land after the reader has already got value.
- Not for: products that need a demo (use P014), long how-tos with steps (use P006), anything that needs numbers or claims to be convincing.

## 4. Structure
4 to 6 slides (6: 9 posts, 5: 8 posts, 4: 3 posts): hook grid → 0 to 3 more grids → explainer with plug → photo closer.

| # | Role | What happens | Text on screen (pattern, not the source's words) |
|---|------|--------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| 1 | Hook grid | Headline + grey reframing subheading, 6 objects that cause it (foods, drinks, habits shown as objects: a phone for "screens in bed", a pillow for "sleeping flat"). | `why [habit] makes you [annoyance]` / `foods that [annoyance verb] your [body part]` / `signs youre [state], not [feared cause]` + sub `its [real cause], not [feared cause]` |
| 2 | Swap grid | 6 better options, each with the reason. | `eat these [when] instead` / `foods that [fix verb] it` / `swap them for these` |
| 3 | Worse grid (optional) | 6 habits or foods that make it worse. | `what makes it [worse / show more]` |
| 4 | Fix grid | 6 habits that fix it, usually with 1 to 3 absurd items mixed in (see §10). | `what actually [fixes / helps / clears] it` |
| n-1 | Explainer + plug | Short heading, pink `...` divider, 2 short mechanism paragraphs, then one first-person line naming the product as how the habit stuck. | `[cause] at [time] shows at [time]` / `its [real cause], not [feared cause]` |
| n | Photo closer | Full-bleed warm lifestyle photo, outcome headline, product as the bio link. | `[outcome], [time or every day]` + `the link in my bio is [product]` |

## 9. Hook formula(s)
- `why [everyday thing] makes you [annoyance]` + sub that reframes it (`its water, not fat, and it leaves`). Mechanic: **reassuring reframe** (it's not your fault, it's not fat).
- `foods that [annoyance verb] your [body part]` + sub with a time hook (`eat them at night, see them at 8am`). Mechanic: **you ate these today** (recognition through the grid).
- `signs youre [state], not [feared cause]` + `if you have three of these, its [state]`. Mechanic: **self-diagnosis checklist**.
- `where your [body part] went` + `its under water, not fat`. Mechanic: **mystery reveal**.
- Numbered variants: `the morning routine that [result]` (steps 1 to 6) and `foods that [annoyance] the most` (ranked 1 to 6).
Reference examples (evidence only): "foods that puff your face / eat them at night, see them at 8am" (282K views), "where your jawline went / its under water, not fat" (63.6K), "why you only go every 3 days / its not normal, and its fixable" (45.8K).

## 10. Slide text rules
- All lowercase headlines; apostrophes dropped (`youre`, `its`, `doesnt`). The second half of every headline is the pink italic payoff.
- Grid labels: 1 to 5 words, sentence case, bold. Reasons: 2 to 6 words, grey (`Salt swells your face overnight`, `Potassium balances salt`).
- Explainer: 50 to 70 words, short lines, second person then first person.
- Closer: 2 to 6 words, outcome plus a time (`at 8am`, `every morning`, `all day`).
- **Comment-bait device:** the fix grid usually hides 1 to 3 absurd "tips" among real ones, given deadpan reasons (`Clap 3 times before water / Every glass`, `Set your alarm for 6:57 / Never 7:00`, `Face east for breakfast / Not a joke`, `Sleep with one foot out / The left one`). They drive comments. They are false health tips, so a brand version must leave them out or replace them with a harmless joke that isn't presented as a fix (rules: no false tips).

## 14. Do / Don't
- Do: keep the grid honest and scannable: 6 objects, each instantly recognisable, one short reason each.
- Do: lead with the reframe ("its water, not fat"); it is what the top posts share.
- Do: make every object look like the same photo shoot (same light, angle, shadow, scale).
- Do: keep the product out of the grids; the plug comes only after the value.
- Don't: copy the absurd comment-bait tips as if they were real advice.
- Don't: copy health claims you can't back up (`a natural diuretic`, `studies back this one`, `pulls water into your gut`). Use only true, general tips.
- Don't: promise body results ("flat stomach", "slim legs", "sharp jawline") for a regulated brand; that is a weight or body-outcome claim.
- Don't: use the source's headlines, object sets or photos (originality check).
