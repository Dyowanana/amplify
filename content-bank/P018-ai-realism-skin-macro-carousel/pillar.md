# P018 – AI realism skin macro carousel

## 1. Source
- https://www.tiktok.com/@alex.prompt/photo/7597462038130085142 (6 slides). Creator @alex.prompt. Collected 2026-10-08. The downloader got the sound only, so the slides were taken from the public photo page in the browser pane (full resolution, 1440×2558).

## 2. Summary
The post claims "this is AI" and then lets the viewer try to prove it wrong. One normal selfie shows the whole person; every other slide is an extreme close-up of her skin with nothing to hide behind.

## 3. Best for
- Showing off the quality of AI people (personas, AI influencers, an AI content tool's output). The texture is the content.
- Any persona with a strong, consistent face (freckles, moles, an iris colour) that holds up at macro distance.
- Not for: health or skincare claims (the skin is not a result of anything), before/after, or any post where the person must be real. The post must say it is AI; that is the hook.

## 4. Structure
6 slides (5 to 8 is fine, keep one context selfie and at least three silent macros).

| # | Role | What happens | Text on screen (pattern) |
|---|------|--------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | Hook | Extreme macro of the most "human" detail (lips, an eye) filling the frame | `[short claim that it is ai]`, 3 to 6 words, the word **ai** in bold |

| 6 | CTA | A last macro (brows, forehead, hairline) with the call to action over it | `[comment keyword or question]`, 2 to 3 lines, keyword in bold |

## 5. Product slot
Only the CTA slide and the caption. The source sells its own offer through a comment keyword ("comment X to …"). No product on the photos. With caption `none`, the CTA is an engagement question instead.

## 6. Proof device
The macros themselves: pores, freckles, peach fuzz, lip lines, stray brow hairs, a soft falloff of focus. The viewer zooms and checks.

## 7. Series structure
Single post. The account's niche (AI influencers) suggests repeat posts with different faces; not confirmed (one post read).

## 9. Hook formula(s)
- `this is [bold ai]` (the formula phrase; trend wording, may stay word for word)
- Variants: `every [pore / freckle / hair] here is [bold ai]` · `[none of this / she] is [bold real]`, then ai in the CTA

**Mechanics:** reveal-by-claim (experience + disbelief). The viewer's eye accepts the image as a photo before reading the line; the line breaks that, and the remaining slides dare them to find the seam. Keep it short and flat in tone; no exclamation, no emoji.

**Reference example (evidence only):** "This is AI"

## 10. Slide text rules
- Text on the hook and the CTA slide only. Slides 2 to 5 have no text.
- Lowercase (owner rule; the source used sentence case), no colon, semicolon, em or en dash.
- One bold word per block (the reveal word or the comment keyword); the rest light/regular weight.
- Hook 3 to 6 words on one line; CTA up to about 12 words on 2 or 3 lines.

## 11. Visual style
**Visual class:** humans. Slide 2 is a **selfie** (guideline 2.17 term 1, no phone in shot). The macros are close-up photos (no selfie term).

- **Canvas:** 9:16 (source 1440×2558).
- **Macros:** the subject fills the frame edge to edge, cropped hard (half a lip, one eye, the side of the nose). Real phone macro look: a thin plane of focus, the edges falling soft, flat soft daylight (no studio light, no rim light), unretouched skin with every pore, freckle, fine hair and dry line. Measured on the source: sharpness 93 to 312, noise 0.32 to 0.51, contrast 16 to 22, saturation 30 to 52.
- **Continuity:** the same outfit colour shows at the edges of the macros (the source: a red hood) and in the selfie, so it reads as one shoot.
- **Selfie:** flat outdoor shade or window light, ordinary place, slight front-camera wide-lens look, direct calm look.
- **Text:** clean white geometric sans (closest Montserrat; in this project Inter at light weight with a bold key word), centred mid-frame on the skin, no box, no outline, at most a very faint soft shadow. Hook about 3.3% of the height, CTA about 2.3%. Measured contrast is low on purpose (3.1 to 4.1); keep it at 3 or more.
- **Fixed:** macro texture as the content, one selfie for context, text on first and last only, white clean type.
- **Varies:** the person, the outfit colour, which features get a macro, the place of the selfie.

## 14. Do / Don't
- Do: make the macros brutally close and unretouched; keep one face across all slides (persona refs on every image); keep the light flat and real; label the post as AI in TikTok.
- Don't: retouch, beautify or add glow; use studio or rim light; put text on the proof slides; copy the source's person, red hood, porch or lips-first order exactly.
- **AI production note:** Nano Banana Pro at 4K with the persona's face, sheet and skin-macro refs; ask for "extreme macro phone photo" and the phone imperfections; downscale to 1080×1920 and apply a light `phone_look()` (strength about 0.5: macros need the texture, a heavy blur kills it). Look at each macro at full size for plastic skin, repeated pore patterns, extra moles or wrong eye colour.
