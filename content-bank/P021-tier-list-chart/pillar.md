# P021 – Tier list chart

## 1. Source
- https://www.tiktok.com/@mentalapp/photo/7688365608508919047 (2 slides, short link `vt.tiktok.com/ZSboCTLHp`). Creator @mentalapp. Collected 2026-10-13. `fetch.py` only saved the music, so the two slides were saved from the public page in the browser pane.

## 2. Summary
A topic cover, then one dense, opinionated tier chart that people screenshot, save and argue with.

## 3. Best for
- Any topic with 10 to 25 familiar options people already have opinions on: habits, tools, channels, foods, apps, strategies.
- Accounts that want comments and saves (the chart is a reference, and the ranking is a debate).
- Not for: step-by-step how-tos, single-product stories, or anything that needs proof per item.

## 4. Structure
| # | Role | What happens | Text on screen (pattern) |
|---|------|--------------|--------------------------|
| 1 | Hook / cover | "tier list of" on top, one vivid hero cut-out in the middle, the topic word big under it, a smaller qualifier word, a line glyph bottom-left and a glossy 3D object bottom-right | `tier list of` / `[Topic]` / `[qualifier]` |
| 2 | Chart | A challenge title top-left, a 5-row table (S+, A, B, C, D) with coloured letter cells and taglines, a plus sign, then a separate F row; 4 cut-out icons with labels per row, the last F cell is a big question mark "your option (comment)"; a CTA bottom-left, the handle bottom-right | `[challenge line]` · `[item label]` × up to 24 · `[CTA]` |

## 5. Product slot
Hidden as one cell in a top tier with "(check bio)" in its label, repeated by the closing line and the caption. With caption style `none` leave the slot out (no bio plug).

## 6. Proof device
None. The ranking is opinion; the colours and the order carry it.

## 7. Series structure
Numbered parts ("pt2" on the cover; the caption says what changed from part 1). Single posts work too; drop the part number.

## 8. Video timing (videos only)
Not applicable (slideshow with a trending song).

## 9. Hook formula(s)
- Cover: `tier list of [topic]` (formula phrase, trend wording, may stay word for word).
- Chart title, mechanic **challenge / dare to disagree**: `prove me wrong` · variants `change my mind`, `fight me on this`, `tell me i'm wrong`.
- F-row closer, mechanic **interactive ask**: a question-mark cell labelled `your option (comment)`.

## 10. Slide text rules
- Lowercase everywhere (the source capitalised the topic word; the owner's rule keeps it lowercase). No dashes, colons or semicolons; commas and slashes are fine.
- Cell labels 1 to 3 words, a fourth word only as a bracket line.
- Tier taglines 2 to 4 words, comma-separated adjectives that explain the tier ("real, earned, lasts").
- One thin grotesque for everything (closest Inter Light, weight 300).

## 11. Visual style
**Visual class:** `other:tier-chart-card`.
- **Canvas** 1080x1350, flat pale background (source #EDF2EB; any very light tint works, keep the lightness about 94%).
- **Cover:** "tier list of" centred at about 17% of the height, cap size about 10%; hero cut-out about 60% wide, 25 to 60% of the height, a loud false-colour or iridescent version of the topic's core object with a thin white outline; topic word at about 66%, about 9% tall; qualifier at about 74%, about 6%; bottom corners a black line glyph (left) and a glossy 3D object (right).
- **Chart table:** x 7.6% to 85.4%, letter column 7.6% to 22%; rows from 17.5% to 73.2% (5 rows, about 11.1% each); a plus sign at about 76%; F row 78.9% to 90%. Hairline black grid. Letter cells red #FB0200, orange #FDA13C, light orange #FADC7C, yellow #FFFD68, light green #BDFD85, F periwinkle #8685ED. Letters about 5.5% tall, taglines about 1.3% at the bottom of the cell.
- **Icons:** cut-outs on the flat card with no shadow, about 8 to 10% tall, the label under or overlapping the icon's foot. Mixed families on purpose: photoreal object cut-outs, glossy 3D emoji-style icons and a few flat black glyphs. No text on the icons.
- **Title** top-left at about 7 to 13%, about 6.3% tall. **CTA** bottom-left at about 95%, **handle** bottom-right, small.
- **Fixed:** the tier colours and order, the plus gap before F, the thin font, the flat card, mixed cut-out icons.
- **Varies:** background tint, topic, items, taglines, hero object.

## 12. Caption formula and hashtag pattern
- One casual lowercase line (what changed since the last part or why this ranking), the topic named, then the CTA.
- 5 hashtags: the topic, its parent topic and a niche community tag.

## 14. Do / Don't
- Do: fill every row (3 to 4 cells), make the top tier the surprising one, end the F row with the question-mark comment cell.
- Don't: put text inside generated images; use a real person's photo as an icon (the source did, never copy that); draw a real brand's logo (name the brand in the label instead).
- **Originality:** new topic, new items and labels, new hero object; the tier structure, colours and the "tier list of" phrase are the format.
- **AI production:** generate icons as sheets of separate objects on white (`gpt25s`), cut them out in code, draw the table and all text in code (`tiercards.py`).
