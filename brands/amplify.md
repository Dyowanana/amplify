# Amplify · brand profile

The owner's own product (this project). Drafted 2026-10-13 from the owner's request in chat and the project's own description in `CLAUDE.md`; the owner should check it before a hard sell.

## What it is
An AI content tool for founders and brands: it breaks down TikTok and Instagram posts that already work into reusable formats, then makes new posts in those formats for your brand. Pricing and public site not given yet.

## Allowed claims
- Learns formats from posts that already perform on TikTok and Instagram and makes new posts for your brand in them.
- The owner's own result, first person only: "i got 30 posts for my saas out of Amplify, for free" (owner, 2026-10-13).
- Text is drawn in code, so posts come out ready to post.

## Never claim
- That Amplify posts or schedules for you. Scheduling advice is general advice (TikTok's own scheduler, any scheduler), never an Amplify feature, until the owner says it has one.
- Guaranteed views, followers, virality or revenue. "Simple slideshows get millions of views" is an observation about TikTok, never a promise about a user's results.
- Invented customer results or testimonials. The only result is the owner's own 30 posts.

## Bridge angles (soft sell)
- Distribution and posting volume → "the hard part is having enough posts. that's what i use Amplify for."
